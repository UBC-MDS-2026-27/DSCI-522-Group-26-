# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, collaborative space where people can contribute and learn. Everyone participating in project discussions, reviews, and activities is expected to help maintain that environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Be open to different backgrounds, experiences, and approaches.
- Respect others' time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, listen to feedback, and correct harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information without permission. Deliberately humiliating or intimidating another participant is also unacceptable.

Disagreement about project decisions is welcome when expressed respectfully. Criticism should address ideas or work, not a person's identity or worth.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project-related events or communications. It applies when someone is representing the project or materially affecting the safety of its participants.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a project maintainer privately through the team's established communication channel. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer or the course staff instead. Include relevant context or links, but do not post private or sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Information may be shared with people who need it to review the concern or take action. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## How concerns are handled

Project maintainers will review reports, hear from the people involved when appropriate, and choose a response proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Responses may include a private reminder, a warning, removal of harmful content, limits on participation, or referral to course staff. Serious or repeated behavior may result in removal from project spaces.

Maintainers are expected to follow this policy too. If you disagree with how a report was handled, you may raise the concern privately with another maintainer or course staff.
